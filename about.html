About SGB Bertrand

Northern roots. Canada-wide capability.

A hands-on modular project team based in Timmins, Ontario and built to support industrial clients wherever the work takes them.

Who we are

Practical leadership for complex modular work.

SGB Bertrand Inc. provides modular sales, project management, construction coordination and site installation for industrial operations across Canada.

We understand that successful modular delivery depends on more than the building itself. It requires careful planning, reliable partners, clear communication and experienced coordination from the first decision to final handover.

Our team works closely with clients to keep those pieces connected and the project moving.
