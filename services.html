Our services

Modular delivery without the gaps.

From procurement to installation, SGB Bertrand brings the complete package together under experienced project leadership.

01

Project Management

Experienced coordination that keeps complex modular projects moving.

We provide a clear point of accountability from early planning through closeout. Our team aligns schedules, contractors, logistics and site requirements so clients can make informed decisions and maintain momentum.

  • Scope and execution planning
  • Schedule and contractor coordination
  • Budget and procurement oversight
  • Site logistics and progress reporting
02

Modular Sales & Procurement

The right modular solution for the site, schedule and operating need.

We help clients identify, source and procure modular facilities for remote and industrial environments. Whether the requirement is new construction or an existing unit, we focus on practical fit, value and readiness for deployment.

  • New and pre-owned modular buildings
  • Vendor sourcing and evaluation
  • Building configuration support
  • Transportation and delivery planning
03

Modular Construction

Purpose-built spaces coordinated for safe, efficient delivery.

SGB Bertrand coordinates the people, materials and services required to move a modular concept into construction. We work with qualified partners to deliver facilities that meet the project’s functional and site-specific requirements.

  • Construction package coordination
  • Trade and supplier management
  • Quality and schedule oversight
  • Industrial and remote-site requirements
04

Site Installation

From delivery through placement, connection and turnover.

Our team manages the field activities needed to receive and install modular buildings at operating sites. We coordinate crane work, assembly, service connections and final closeout around the realities of the site.

  • Site readiness coordination
  • Transportation and crane planning
  • Placement and module assembly
  • Connection, closeout and handover
